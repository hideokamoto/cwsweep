This site (https://cwsweep.hidetaka.dev) uses Google Analytics to learn which pages people read so the documentation can be improved. This page explains what is sent for that purpose and how to stop it.

A Japanese version of this page is available at [プライバシーポリシー（外部送信について）](/ja/privacy/).

## What is sent

When you open a page, the Google Analytics program sends the following to Google:

- The URL and title of the page you opened, and when you opened it (only query parameters starting with `utm_`, which show where a visit came from, are kept; all others are removed before sending)
- The page you came from (its path if it is a page on this site, otherwise only the other site's domain)
- Your device, OS, and browser type, screen size, and language setting
- An approximate location estimated from your IP address
- An identifier used to count your visits to this site together (stored in a browser cookie)

Nothing that directly identifies you, such as your name or email address, is sent.

## Recipient

- Google LLC (service: Google Analytics)

## Purpose

- **The operator of this site**: aggregates page views, the share of English and Japanese readers, and similar figures to improve the documentation.
- **Google LLC**: according to Google, it "uses the information shared by sites, apps and partners to deliver our services, maintain and improve them, develop new services, measure the effectiveness of advertising, protect against fraud and abuse, and depending on your settings, personalize content and ads". See [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) for how Google Analytics collects and processes data.

## How to stop it

Install the [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout) provided by Google to stop sending data to Google Analytics. Deleting or blocking cookies in your browser settings also removes the identifier used to count your visits together. You can keep using this site after opting out.
